Verify a Certificate

DU Risk-E-Scape Certificate Course

Enter the certificate ID printed on the certificate, including the four characters after the last dash. Scanning the QR code goes straight to the record.

Certificates cannot be looked up by name — a record is reachable only by its certificate ID.
Cannot find a certificate you believe is genuine? Contact riskescape.du@gmail.com.
