Improving Disaster Health and Climate Resilience

A certificate course on disaster response, humanitarian health and community resilience, delivered by Department of Development Studies, University of Dhaka as part of the EU co-funded RISK-E-SCAPE project.

About the course

The course brings together disaster response, humanitarian medicine, displacement, leadership and community engagement in a single programme. It is intended for professionals and graduates working in development, disaster management, public health, engineering and the social sciences, and is taught in English and Bengali.

Consortium

RISK-E-SCAPE is delivered by a consortium of universities across Bangladesh, Nepal, Greece, Italy and Portugal.
