Why the ID and URL are printed as text, not just encoded in the QR: the QR encodes baseUrl from config.json, and that address is frozen the moment these are printed. If the account or repo behind it ever goes away, the QR is dead — but a human can still read the ID and look it up wherever the verification site lives by then. The text block is the credential's survival path.
