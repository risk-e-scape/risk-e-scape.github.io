Certificate records

Privacy and published information

This page explains what is shown when the {{FULL_TITLE}} certificate is verified and how to request a correction.

What a record contains

An issued certificate record contains the participant name printed on the certificate, certificate ID, batch, issue date and a link to the certificate PDF. A revoked record keeps the certificate ID and revocation notice online but does not provide the PDF.

Why records are published

Publication allows a certificate holder, employer or other verifier with the certificate ID to confirm that the credential was issued by {{HOST}}. Records cannot be searched by name and certificate pages are excluded from search-engine indexing and the public sitemap.

Anyone who receives a certificate ID or scans its QR code can access the corresponding record. Certificate holders should therefore share their certificate and ID deliberately.

Source-data protection

The participant roster used to generate records is not stored in this public website repository. It must contain only information printed on the certificate; contact details, identity numbers, grades, marks, addresses and dates of birth are not accepted by the build.

Corrections and questions

To report an incorrect name, date, certificate status or document link, contact course coordination. Include the certificate ID, but do not send sensitive identity documents by email unless the coordination office specifically requests a secure verification method.
