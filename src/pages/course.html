Course overview

The course prepares participants to work effectively in disaster and climate emergencies, combining disaster response principles with humanitarian health practice, the realities of displacement, and the leadership and community skills that field work demands. Teaching uses taught sessions, group work and practical exercises, and each part is assessed.

Who it is for

Professionals and graduates in development studies, disaster management, public health, engineering and the social sciences.

Teaching languages

English and Bengali.

Certification

Certificates are issued to participants who meet the approved completion requirements for their course delivery. Contact course coordination for the assessment and eligibility criteria.

What the course covers

Disaster response

Core principles, the disaster management cycle, and how risk and vulnerability are assessed.

Humanitarian medicine

Delivering health care in emergencies, under pressure and under constraint.

People on the move

Displacement and climate migration, and protection for people affected by them.

Leadership and communication

Working and communicating across organisations during hazards and overlapping crises.

Community engagement

Building resilience with affected communities rather than for them.
