Overview

The course prepares participants to work effectively in disaster and climate emergencies, combining the principles of disaster response with humanitarian health practice, the realities of displacement, and the leadership and community skills that field work demands. Teaching is a mix of taught sessions, group work and practical exercises, and each part of the course is assessed.

What the course covers

Disaster response

Core principles, the disaster management cycle, and how risk and vulnerability are assessed.

Humanitarian medicine

Delivering health care in emergencies, under pressure and under constraint.

People on the move

Displacement and climate migration, and protection for people affected by them.

Leadership and communication

Working and communicating across organisations during hazards and overlapping crises.

Community engagement

Building resilience with affected communities rather than for them.

Who it is for

Professionals and graduates in development studies, disaster management, public health, engineering and the social sciences. Teaching is in English and Bengali.
